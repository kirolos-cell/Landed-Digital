Case study · The Golden Fade Barbershop

16 Google reviews in week one for a brand-new shop.

The challenge. The Golden Fade is a growing barbershop with two Melbourne shops. Their established Caroline Springs shop was doing well, but each new location starts from zero on Google.

What we did. We set up and optimised their Google Business Profile and put a simple tap-to-review system in the hands of their team, so asking for a review became part of the cut.

The result. Caroline Springs has grown past 200 genuine reviews. When they opened in Oakleigh South, the brand-new shop reached 16 reviews in its first week of trading.
