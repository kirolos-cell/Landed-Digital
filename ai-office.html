questions

AI, without the hype.

What can an AI receptionist do?
Answer common questions, capture enquiry details, respond to missed calls, help book appointments and pass real leads to your team. It works around your existing process.
Will AI replace my staff?
No. Our AI handles the repetitive load so your team can spend time on the work that needs a person. There is always human oversight.
Can the AI system work with my current tools?
In most cases, yes. We connect to common tools and can integrate with your CRM or booking system where it makes sense. We start simple and expand only if it helps.
Where should we start?
With one clear use case, usually missed calls or repeat enquiries. We prove the value on that, then look at the next opportunity.
