## Rules while submitting

1. **Never vary the name.** `Landed Digital` exactly. Not "Landed Digital Melbourne",
   not "Landed Digital - Google Reviews". Keyword-stuffed names get listings suspended
   and break the NAP match.
2. **Same phone everywhere**, formatted `0403 385 125`.
3. **Same URL everywhere**, with `https://` and no trailing slash inconsistency.
4. **Do not buy paid placements** in these directories. The free listing carries the
   citation value; the paid upsell rarely pays for itself at this stage.
5. **Record what you submit** in the tracker below, because duplicates are harder to
   remove than to avoid.
6. **Expect verification** — many will phone or email the listed contact.
