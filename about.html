the story

Why we started

This business grew out of practical work. Helping local businesses with Google Ads, tidying up Google Business Profiles, building and fixing websites, and setting up NFC review systems and general digital setup.

The same pattern kept showing up. A business needed help across several connected areas at once, but was forced to deal with a separate agency for ads, a freelancer for the website, someone else for reviews and yet another tool for automation. Nobody owned the whole picture, and the owner was left stitching it all together.

Landed Digital exists to be the one practical partner that joins those pieces up: Google, reviews, websites and AI, under one point of contact, explained in plain English.

We make businesses easier to find, easier to choose and easier to run.

The whole business, in one sentence.
