Yes. Eligible Australian businesses get one programmed card, a QR code and a direct review link set up at no cost. No payment details needed. Extra cards, stands and ongoing review services are separate.
Does it work on iPhone and Android?
Modern iPhones and most Android phones read NFC. Every card also has a QR code, so any smartphone camera can use it too.
Do I have to sign up to anything?
No. It is a genuine free first step. If it is helpful and you want more cards, stands or ongoing help, those are available separately, but there is no obligation.
Will this get me fake reviews?
No, and we would not help with that. This only makes it easier for real customers to leave genuine, voluntary reviews. We never gate, filter or incentivise reviews.
