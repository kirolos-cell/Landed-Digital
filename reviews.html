We only help you collect genuine, voluntary reviews.

Google reviews must be honest and freely given. questions

Review cards, answered.

Does the review card work with iPhone and Android?
Modern iPhones and most Android phones read NFC. Every card also carries a QR code as a backup, so any smartphone camera can use it.
Can the card be reprogrammed?
Yes. Cards can be re-pointed if your details change, and additional cards and counter stands are available.
Do you offer multiple cards and stands?
Yes. Start with one, then add cards for each station or staff member and counter stands for the front desk.
Can you help multiple business locations?
Yes. We set up a consistent review system across every location and report on them together.
Is the NFC setup really free?
Yes. Eligible Australian businesses get one programmed card, a QR code and a direct review link set up at no cost. Extra cards, stands and ongoing review services are separate.
